PROTOTYPE · throwaway

The Parent phone

M9 ticket 05. Everything above the switcher is the shared flow: role choice, account, pairing. The per-Child-phone screens come in three structurally different variants; flip with the bar at the bottom, ← → keys, or ?variant=A|B|C.

A · Espejo: the Child's own Status, mirrored after a fetch. B · Tarjetas: every Child on one page as small state cards with Start/Stop on them. C · Pedir: no guessed state, a menu of requests that each come back with an answer.

The open question under all three: pure relay means the Parent phone knows nothing until it asks. A and B ask on open and show a state stamped "hace 3 s"; C never shows a state it was not just told.

First launch, account and pairing. Same in every variant.
